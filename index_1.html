Before / after

Drag the squeegee.

A north-facing bay window in the Berkeley flats after one winter. Salt film, redwood pollen, and sprinkler overspray along the bottom rail.

Before After
01

Salt film

West-facing glass within a couple miles of the shoreline hazes over in about six weeks. It's not dirt — it's a mineral layer, and it needs surfactant, not a hose.

02

Bottom-rail spotting

Sprinklers throw calcium onto the lower six inches. Caught early it wipes off. Left for two summers it etches into the glass permanently.

03

Redwood pollen

Late spring, every year, all over North Berkeley and Kensington. It's sticky enough that a dry wipe just grinds it into the surface.

Questions

The stuff people actually ask.

Do I need to be home?

Not for exterior-only work — we just need access to the side gate and an outdoor spigot. For inside-and-out we do need someone there to let us in, and we'll give you a two-hour arrival window the day before rather than an all-day one.

What happens if it rains right after?

Rain on clean glass doesn't actually dirty it — what leaves spots is dirt already sitting on the frame washing down. If your windows look worse within seven days of a job, text us a photo and we come back and redo the affected panes at no charge.

Can you reach a third story on a hillside lot?

Yes. Water-fed carbon poles reach about 40 feet from stable ground, so most El Cerrito and Kensington hillside homes are done without a ladder ever touching the house. If a face genuinely can't be reached safely, we'll tell you before booking instead of finding out on the day.

Will hard-water stains actually come off?

Usually. Light mineral spotting comes off with an acidic gel and a lot of dwell time. Deep etching — where the mineral has eaten into the glass — needs a cerium oxide polish, and some of it is permanent. We test one pane first and tell you honestly what's recoverable before charging for the whole house.

Do you clean stained glass, leaded glass, or old wavy panes?

Yes, and carefully — no pressure, no ammonia, no scrapers. Original Victorian glass in the Berkeley flats is thin and already stressed. Flag it in the notes and we'll hand-clean those sashes separately.

How do I pay?

Card, Zelle, Venmo or check, after the work is done and you've looked at it. No deposit for residential jobs. Storefront routes are invoiced monthly, net 15.

Are you insured?

Licensed, bonded and carrying general liability plus workers' comp. Certificates go out with every quote, and property managers can get us added as an additional insured on request.
